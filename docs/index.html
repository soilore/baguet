[ EXHIBITION 06 // MATHEMATICAL INTEGRITY ]

Cryptographic Verification.

Every operation produces an Ed25519 signed audit certificate guaranteeing 100% bit-exact roundtrip reproducibility.

Baguet maps IEEE 754 float sign, exponent, and mantissa into orthogonal bit-planes. Search and dot-product compute only need the most significant planes to prune unpromising candidates, calculating Top-K sketches without ever reconstructing full uncompressed tensors.
All decompression, untranscode, and mmap reader algorithms are open and free forever under MIT license. You can extract your raw data at any time without paying license fees.
